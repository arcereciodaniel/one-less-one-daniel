# V15.6b — transition preflight and preparation (not activation)

The legacy migration deduplicates by budgetMonth/fortnight, and the filters, recurring income and forecast assume two periods per month. Inserting flexible periods now risks merging or mislabeling records. Do not materialize them in data.periods yet.

The existing calendar settings dialog contains a collapsed transition section. Preflight computes the first future day after the existing budget horizon, rejects overlap and gaps, and blocks future movements explicitly assigned to old periods. The user may prepare a transition only after confirmation; a single verified local write stores its proposed frequency, dates and source boundary signature. The signature is rechecked on opening the dialog and may become review-required if dates or assignments change. Withdrawal removes only the prepared transition. No live periods, income projections, movement assignments, or financial totals change. The prepared transition never auto-activates. Canonical variable-length period identity, migration and every dependent consumer must be upgraded and tested before activation; this is unfinished V15.6. No CSS or deployment changes; real ledger and V14 reconciliation remain unverified.
