# V13.1 · Native flexible budgets (Beta)

Enable flexible views under Settings. In Budget choose week, fortnight, month or quarter, navigate to the current or future period and Create budget. Entries use original CRC/USD. Income suggestions use the category's actual scheduled payment dates (not legacy assignment), are snapshots and can be edited. Compatible legacy plans can seed amounts; mismatched plans are not prorated. Native ranges cannot overlap and are listed under Mis presupuestos. Exact native ranges take precedence over the overlapping legacy allocation in flexible reporting; larger ranges only consolidate fully covered plans. The original ledger, legacy plans, and current dashboard/future projections are not migrated. Restore validates range overlap, dates and entries; existing local keys are unchanged. New native plans are not included in old projections until a later migration.
