# V14.4 / V14.5 · Living Forecast and safe transition

Futuro uses authoritative V13 plan sources and dated V14 replacements. Ordinary expenses and goal contributions consume the remaining source-level budget after registered activity; allocation weights reflect effective dated budgets, with a fallback uniform estimate if no positive weights remain. Debt minimums are scheduled first; additional budgeted debt amounts are estimated separately. Override dates in gaps are included as partial known commitments without inventing full coverage. In Living mode configured income dates are independent of budget cuts and confirmed income reduces scheduled amounts on the same date. Legacy-only forecasts preserve prior behavior. Scenarios consume the same forecast. No ledger mutations. Historical editor stays reachable; universal is initial view for users with native/dated plans or preference enabled. All date filters are always accessible. Restore validates and counts both native and dated plans; duplicate or corrupt entries are rejected rather than silently removed. Same browser Lab snapshot remains independent. Mobile capture still requires legacy period IDs until a separate safe migration. Actual cash availability estimates exclude future income, while Futuro includes dated scheduled income. No automatic category frequency configuration or destructive migration.
